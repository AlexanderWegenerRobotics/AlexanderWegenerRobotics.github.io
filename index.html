Research question

Does guiding the operator produce better training data?

Policies are only as good as the demonstrations, and through a teleoperation rig people act worse than in person: they cannot feel contact, they see the past, and the interface loads them. Nudging changes what the operator perceives, not what the robot does, so the logged action stays purely human.

Munich showed nudging helps the operator. The current system predicts operator intent live and renders cues from it; the next study trains one policy on nudged demonstrations and one on unassisted ones and compares task success. That study is in design — the human-gated takeover above is its comparator.
